# Privacy Policy

*Inbuilt Video Editor for X (Twitter)* — last updated 28 September 2026

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- Videos you open are processed entirely inside your browser, using WebCodecs. They are never uploaded to any server by the extension.
- The extension makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- The extension requests no browser permissions. Its content script runs only on `x.com` and `twitter.com`, where it adds an **Edit video** button to the post composer and places the edited clip in the composer's own file input. Nothing is uploaded until you choose to post on X, and then X's own terms and privacy policy apply.
- Nothing is saved between sessions.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/danialbka/twitter-editor-inbuilt/issues
